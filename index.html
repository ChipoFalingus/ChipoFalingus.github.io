ChipoFling's Colony Game

#1 What Is This Game? | 08/15/2026

Before showcasing any features or diving into any behind-the-scenes stuff, I figured I should describe what this game even is and what to expect from it. This game is an ASCII-based colony simulator inspired by genre-defining titles like Dwarf Fortress and RimWorld.

What makes this one different?

I want my game to blend together colony building and RTS elements together. You're tasked with standard colony game stuff, like keeping your colonists happy and fed. You also must defend your colony from rampant swarms of zombies that will attempt to destroy your colony. Give weapons to your colonists and build walls to keep zombies out. I also want to add an exploration mechanic where you can send a squad of your colonists to loot procedurally-generated locations.

Why a colony simulator?

Making a colony simulator seemed like a fun and interesting project. I've watched videos documenting the development of the other games mentioned and became interested in building an unpredictable simulation. It's fun adding new rules and watching them interact with the rest of the system in interesting ways.

Why ASCII?

Developing a game with ASCII graphics speeds up development as I'm not being slowed down by graphics fidelity. There's also an aesthetic about the simplicity of colored characters that I like. I have plans for graphics upgrades and/or custom ASCII characters later down the line, but that will be a while from now.

I'll dive into more technical details starting next post, but this should give a better idea of what the end goal is and what the systems are trying to accomplish.
